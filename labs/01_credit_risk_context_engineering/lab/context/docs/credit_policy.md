# Charge-off and Recovery Policy (synthetic, for training)

Owner: Second Line Credit Risk · Applies to: consumer lending (Cards, Personal Loan, Auto)

## Charge-off timing
Our credit union charges off consumer loans EARLIER than the regulatory maximum:
- **Auto loans** are charged off when they reach **90 days past due** (or at repossession sale, if sooner).
- **Personal loans** (unsecured) are charged off at **120 days past due**.
- **Credit cards** are charged off at **150 days past due**.

A charged-off account gets status CO and Metro 2 code 97. Its balance is written off and is no longer part of the outstanding portfolio.

## Recoveries
Recoveries are collected for up to six months after charge-off. Expected recovery: about 45% for Auto (collateral sale), 18% for Personal Loans and 12% for Cards.
Net charge-offs = gross charge-offs minus recoveries.

## Who decides
Charge-off decisions are operational and follow this policy. Credit decisions (approve, decline, price) are made by underwriting staff. They are not made by analytics tools or AI assistants.
